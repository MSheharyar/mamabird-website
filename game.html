Chirpy's First Flight

Chirpy is learning to fly, just like in the book. Tap to flap, and help him find the gaps between the branches.

How to play

  • TapTap the picture on a phone or tablet.
  • SpaceOr press the space bar on a keyboard.
  • ↑The up arrow works too.

Start on Gentle. Switch to Brisk when that feels too easy — the buttons are on the start screen.

For grown-ups: the game runs entirely in this browser. There is no sign-in, no adverts and nothing is sent anywhere — the best score is remembered on this device only.

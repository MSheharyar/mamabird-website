The real question

You didn’t want another screen.
You wanted them learning.

Fair. Most things aimed at small children are built to hold their attention, not to teach them anything. Here is the difference, plainly.

Passive screen time

The app decides

  • Something else always starts playing.
  • Your child watches rather than answers.
  • You find out what they saw afterwards, if at all.
  • Adverts are how it pays for itself.
Chirpy’s Classroom

Your child decides

  • Nothing happens until your child asks a question.
  • Answers are written for the age you set — 2 to 8.
  • Every session is saved for you to read later.
  • No feed, no adverts, and leaving needs your PIN.

About the Author

Iris & Frank Scarfone

Iris Scarfone and her husband Frank have always believed the love of reading begins at home. When their son Ronald was a baby, they taught him to read by placing words on posterboards around the house.

That hands-on philosophy became the heartbeat of Three Baby Birdies — short, rhythmic language with illustrations that let young children follow and understand the story on their own terms.

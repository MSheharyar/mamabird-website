Iris & Frank Scarfone

When their son Ronald was a baby, Iris and Frank taught him to read by putting words on posterboards around the house. Reading, to them, has always started at home.

That is the whole idea behind Three Baby Birdies: short, rhythmic language and pictures clear enough that a child who cannot read yet can still follow the story.

Illustrated and educationally informed by Ronald Scarfone, their son
